You are adding a new playable character, **Teo1910** (written テオ1910 in Japanese), to **Hoodratz**, a real-time 2D stickman fighting game inspired by YOMI Hustle. The project is in `haze-vs-rip/` (the folder still has the game's old name; don't rename it). Read `README.md` first: it documents every system and the full controls. ## Ground rules

- Plain JavaScript in classic `<script>` tags (no modules, no build step); the game must run by opening `index.html` from `file://`. HTML5 canvas, 1280x720.
- Game logic runs in fixed 60 Hz ticks and is **deterministic: never use `Math.random` in game logic** (render-only effects may). Frame counts are logic ticks. Anything that looks random (stumbling, sway) must come from frame counters and sine waves.
- Put all tuning numbers in `src/config.js` (a new `TEO` object), not inline.
- Match the existing code style: short comments explaining *why*, the same naming and idioms as the other kits.
- **Do not break anything.** All existing checks in `node tests/run.cjs` must still pass, plus your new ones. Other characters (YittyMack is in; Albino Rino, Lancer and Blue Cheese may be) must keep working. Reuse what's there: the projectile system (`src/projectiles.js`), floor-object logic like Rip's `g.creams`, and any per-character draw scale or height.
- **Fantasy-game inspiration, original designs.** Teo loves a famous snowy fantasy RPG about a dragon-slaying hero. The game must **not** use that game's names, shouts, characters, creatures, items, logos or music. Everything is original, in the same spirit: a hooded Nordic adventurer, a tavern, a roaring "shout" (called a **Bellow** here), loot. Likewise **no real drink brand names or labels**: his bottles are generic amber-glass cognac bottles with a plain blank label.
- **Tone:** cartoon tavern slapstick. Drinking and smoking are cartoon flavour only: a puff of smoke, a hiccup, wobbling stars. **No blood, no gore**; a bottle that "bashes a head" is classic slapstick: it shatters into harmless glass sparkles, the victim sees spinning stars, and they stay a normal stickman.
- **Teo is not Haze.** Haze already owns the smoking theme (Smoke Weed, smoke clouds). Teo's smoking is a tiny cigarette-style puff used for one small ability (Smoke Break); don't reuse Haze's smoke-cloud, "High" or "feels nothing" mechanics.

## Current game systems you must support (this prompt was updated for them)

The game has grown since this character was first designed. It now has a Medium attack, a tap/hold Heavy, an air-combo game, a separate Burst meter and CPU opponents. **Read the README sections on them first**, then make sure the new fighter works with every one. A fighter that ignores them will crash or feel broken.

1. **Medium attack (a new button).** Medium is a shared move (`MOVES.medium`: 9-frame startup, 65 damage, longer reach than Light, blockable and high-parried). Keys: V (1P), K or Numpad 4 (2P), A on a controller. **Its animation is looked up per fighter**: in `attackAnim` (`src/animation.js`) a `variants` object is indexed by `f.stats.id`. A missing entry makes the pose undefined and the game **crashes the first time he uses Medium**, so you must add an entry for `teo` (a pose in the same style as the existing four; see "Medium and air flavour" below).
2. **Heavy is tap or hold.** A tap throws `heavyTap` (12-frame startup, 110 damage, short knockback); holding the button for 10 frames throws the full `heavy` (28-frame startup, 140 damage, wall launcher). Both are unblockable and unparryable and share the 1.5 s cooldown (`MOVE.heavyCooldown`). Both use your `teo_heavy` signature timeline (`heavyTap` maps to the `heavy` animation), so check that the timeline looks right for the short version and for a heavy chained off a hit, which skips its first 10 frames.
3. **Air game.** In the air there are Air Light (`air`, uses your `teo_air` signature), Air Medium (`airMedium`) and Air Heavy (`airHeavy` / `airHeavyTap`), each usable once per jump (`airMovesUsed`) and cancelling into each other on hit. The Air Medium and Air Heavy animations are shared (no per-fighter work needed), but their poses must still look right on your body. **Up + Light** (the `overhead` move) now **launches** on a clean hit, once per combo; blocking and armor prevent the launch. The combo limit is **10 hits** (7 before a wall bounce, 3 after), with damage scaling down each hit (`COMBO_SCALE`).
4. **Burst** is its own meter now (`f.burstMeter`, one charge that refills over 30 seconds of fighting). It costs no ultimate. Block + Parry while being hit (controller: LB + RT) launches the attacker away. Don't add any new meter or ability that bypasses or conflicts with it, and any new "untouchable", armored or locked state you add must be handled in `burst()` / `tryBurst` in the same way existing ones are.
5. **Controls and HUD limits.** There are now 7 action buttons (Light, Medium, Parry, Grab, Heavy, Special, Block); **don't add any**. Controller: X Light, A Medium, B Grab, Y Heavy, RB Special, LB Block, **RT Parry**. The HUD shows at most 3 ability icons and a small Burst bar beside the ultimate bar.
6. **CPU opponents.** Any fighter can be picked as a CPU opponent at levels 1-5 (`src/cpu.js`). It reads the **committed moves** of the player, never their held buttons, and chooses abilities like this: it picks randomly from `['special', 'downSpecial', 'upSpecial']`, keeping those where `f.kit[key]` exists and `f.cooldowns[key]` is 0, and only when the opponent is closer than 260 px (650 for Yitty). Haze's `upSpecial` (a hold ability) is excluded. **You must keep that convention working for `teo`**:
   - Use those three slot keys, and keep `f.cooldowns[<slotKey>]` meaningful, because that's what the CPU reads.
   - If a move doesn't fit the generic rules (hold or toggle abilities, long-range projectiles, setups that need positioning, moves that should only be used when a bar is full), add an `teo`-specific case in `cpu.js`, as Haze and Yitty already have, and say what you did.
   - `tests/cpu.js` loops over the whole `ROSTER`, so the new fighter is automatically tested at every CPU level: **each level must close distance and deal damage with him**, and CPU decisions must stay deterministic (the CPU uses its own seeded RNG; never `Math.random`).
7. **Practice mode and round reset.** Practice's "infinite resources" refills cooldowns (`special`, `downSpecial`, `upSpecial`, `heavy`) and Burst; refill or reset your new meters there too where it makes sense. Anything new on the fighter or in `g` must be reset in `reset()` / `startRound()`.

## The character

**Teo1910** (`id: 'teo'`, display name `TEO1910`; show `テオ1910` as the subtitle under the name on the player-select screen **only if** the canvas font renders it: test it, and fall back to just `TEO1910` otherwise): a stickman adventurer who is part drunken brawler, part sticky-fingered looter. He's cheerful, loud and a little unsteady.

### Look

- Stickman body in leather brown (`color: '#c98f5a'`, `dark: '#7a5330'`; mirror-match alt `altColor: '#8fc0a0'`, `altDark: '#4f7a62'`).
- A **hood with a small fur collar** (a filled hood shape around the head and a scruffy fur-trim line at the neck), and a **belt with a small bottle hanging from it** (it fills the HUD's bottle count: draw one amber bottle per bottle he's holding, up to 3).
- Idle: a gentle, constant sway that grows with his tipsy stage (see below). Walk: a rolling, wobbly gait. Run: a loose, lurching sprint.
- **Tipsy tint (render-only):** buzzed gets rosy cheeks (two small pink dots on the head); drunk adds wobbling hand and head offsets and a swirl of tiny stars; blackout swirls spiral eyes.

### Stats (`CHARACTERS.teo`)

`walk: 3.7, walkBack: 3.1, runMax: 11.4, dmg: 1.0, weight: 1.05`. Standard height.
Home on the player-select map: `{ u: 0.45, v: 0.13, label: 'TAVERN · REYKJAVIK' }` (put the pin where it doesn't overlap other pins, and nudge it if it does). Title `'The drunken looter'`, blurb `'Pockets full of other people's stuff, belly full of everything else. Surprisingly hard to hit.'`. Add `'teo'` to `ROSTER`.

### Core mechanic: LOOT and TIPSY

**Loot:** every hit Teo lands (a normal, a Pickpocket or the grab) **steals a bottle**: a small amber bottle pops out of the opponent in the hit effect and flies to his belt. He holds up to **3 bottles**. Show them as small bottle icons under his ability icons (`drawCooldowns` in `render.js`, around `y = 194`), next to the TIPSY bar.

**TIPSY (0-100)** is his drunkenness, shown as a bar under his ability icons with the stage name written on it:

| Stage | TIPSY | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| **Sober** | 0-24 | Normal fighter. |
| **Buzzed** | 25-59 | +10% move speed and +10% damage. A light, happy sway. |
| **Drunk** | 60-89 | +20% damage. **Armor:** ordinary hits (not heavies or grabs) don't flinch him, with Rip's Relentless-style armor (see `rip.js` and `landHit` in `combat.js`), but his attacks have a slightly **wobbly angle**: the hitbox offset follows a frame-based sine, ±12 px vertically (deterministic), so highs and lows are less predictable. His movement is drifty (±0.8 px/frame lateral sway). |
| **Blackout** | 90-100 | At 100 he **blacks out**: he sways and sits, open for 40 frames, and TIPSY resets to 50. A "BLACKOUT" popup. He can't be armored while blacked out. |

**TIPSY drains** at -4 per second while he has no bottle to drink and isn't doing anything (so he has to keep looting). Drinking (Chug) is how he raises it on purpose. It resets each round.

### Abilities: the three slots change with his tipsy stage

The three ability slots (Special / Down+Special / Up+Special) are **different moves depending on whether he is below or above 60 TIPSY** (Sober/Buzzed vs Drunk/Blackout). The HUD icons change their label to match, with a short flash when the stage flips. Add an optional per-kit hook for this (for example `labelFor(f, key)` and `abilityFor(f, key)`), and make `trySpecial` in `fighter.js` and `drawCooldowns` use it; keep the other characters' behaviour identical. Cooldowns are **per ability**, not per slot: keep a cooldown map by ability name so switching stage doesn't reset or dodge them.

| Slot | Sober / Buzzed (under 60) | Drunk / Blackout (60+) |
|---|---|---|
| Special | **Chug** | **Bottle Throw** |
| Down + Special | **Pickpocket** | **Stumble Dodge** |
| Up + Special | **Smoke Break** | **Bellow** |

- **Chug** (label `'CHUG'`, cooldown 2 s): needs at least 1 bottle. A 20-frame open animation: he downs a bottle with a gulp and a hiccup. **+25 TIPSY**, uses the bottle. No bottle means it doesn't start (a small "EMPTY" popup).
- **Bottle Throw** (label `'THROW'`, cooldown 3 s): uses a bottle. A 12-frame wind-up, then he hurls it as a projectile (`spawnProjectile`, new `kind: 'bottle'`, speed 11, tumbling). It hits **high**; a high parry knocks it away, block costs 6 guard. 45 damage, 16 hitstun. On hit or on reaching the floor it **shatters** into harmless glass sparkles and leaves a **booze puddle** (reuse the floor-object idea from Rip's cream) that slows whoever stands in it by 25% for 4 s. The puddle does not slow Teo.
- **Pickpocket** (label `'PICK'`, cooldown 4 s): a fast low lunge (hitbox like a low, 7 startup, 3 active, 18 recovery) that does **no damage** but steals: **15 of their ultimate meter** (added to his own) and **1 bottle**. A parry stops it (it's a low, so a low parry). Whiff recovery as listed.
- **Stumble Dodge** (label `'DODGE'`, cooldown 5 s): a drunken sway: frames 3-14 he's **low-profile and invulnerable to highs and lows** (not grabs or heavies), then he lurches forward with a **counter shoulder-bump** (60 damage, launches). If nothing was dodged, he just stumbles and is open for 20 frames. Costs nothing.
- **Smoke Break** (label `'BREAK'`, cooldown 8 s): he lights a cigarette (30 frames, open) with a tiny, cartoon puff (do **not** reuse Haze's smoke clouds): **freezes TIPSY drain for 4 s** and **recovers 20 guard**. It's his way to hold a stage steady.
- **Bellow** (label `'BELLOW'`, cooldown 7 s): a roared shout (original name and look; a ring of sound waves with a "FUS"-free, original word popup such as "BELLOWWW!"). 24-frame wind-up, then a short-range shockwave that hits in a cone about 260 px in front of him. 50 damage, **big knockback** (a wall bounce is possible), blockable (costs 12 guard) and parryable as a high. At 90+ TIPSY the knockback is 1.5x.

Cooldown labels and key hints on the HUD show the current slot's ability. The inputs follow the existing rules: `trySpecial` in `fighter.js` picks downSpecial/upSpecial/special from the held direction, and the jumpsquat conversion already makes Up + Special work for kits that have `upSpecial`.
